IFC models used by the reference cases
======================================

All files are unmodified copies. MODELS.json maps each model path stored in
ground_truth/*.json to its file here and records its SHA-256; ground_truth.py
resolves the stored paths through it.

ec3/  (models of the EC3-2026 paper, copied from its repository)
  Source  : https://github.com/stefan-1992/ACC-function-generation, acc/bim_models/<name>/arc.ifc
            (Fuchs, Hellin and Borrmann 2026); repository licence in ACC-function-generation_LICENSE.txt
  dental_clinic.ifc : BSI (2020) "Medical-Dental Test Files", buildingSMART International. CC BY 4.0.
  duplex.ifc        : BSI (2020) "Duplex Apartment Test Files", buildingSMART International. CC BY 4.0.
  fzk_house.ifc     : IAI / Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), KIT IFC Examples. Unrestricted use.
  smiley_west.ifc   : IAI / Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), KIT IFC Examples. Unrestricted use.
  Per-model notices: <name>_license.txt.

heldout/  (additional public models of the held-out test set)
  Esplanades, Schependomlaan, kleine Wohnung and NUS school: sources, licences, required
  attribution and SHA-256 in heldout/SOURCES_AND_CITATIONS.txt. Schependomlaan: data owners
  permit scientific and academic use only (schependomlaan_license.txt). kleine Wohnung and
  NUS: public test files of the web-ifc repository, no model-specific licence; distributed
  under the repository's MPL-2.0 (kleine_wohnung_license.txt, nus_skolebygg_license.txt,
  full licence text in engine_web-ifc_LICENSE.txt).

bca/  (Singapore BCA test models used by the development cases; local only)
  Not redistributed: there is no permission to publish these models. They are not in the
  public repository; MODELS.json marks them "distributed": false and the BCA reference
  cases are excluded from publication. Local source: the non-public bca_tests repository,
  BCATestCases/<check>/Test models/. The copies under bca_tests-main and
  bca_tests-harness_improvements were byte-identical and are stored once.
